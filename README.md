# UniSin

**The Next-Generation Music Streaming Platform**

UniSin is a modern, feature-rich music streaming platform that brings together artists, creators, and music lovers in one seamless experience. Built with cutting-edge technology, UniSin offers unlimited music streaming, personalized discovery, and powerful creator tools.

## 🎵 What is UniSin?

UniSin is a comprehensive music streaming service that allows users to:

- **Stream unlimited music** - Access millions of songs, albums, and playlists
- **Discover new music** - Personalized recommendations based on your taste
- **Create and share playlists** - Curate your own collections and share with friends
- **Follow your favorite artists** - Stay updated with new releases
- **Upload your own music** - Apply to become a creator and share your art with the world
